Custard

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp custard powder
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla essence

Steps

  1. Heat milk in a pan over medium flame.
  2. Mix custard powder with a little cold milk to form a paste.
  3. Add sugar to the hot milk and stir.
  4. Slowly add the custard paste, stirring continuously until thick.
  5. Add vanilla essence, cool, and refrigerate before serving.

Choco Lava Cake

Ingredients

  • 100g dark chocolate
  • 100g butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 50g sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour

Steps

  1. Melt chocolate and butter together.
  2. Whisk eggs and sugar until fluffy, then fold in the chocolate mix.
  3. Gently fold in the flour.
  4. Pour into greased ramekins and bake at 200°C for 10-12 minutes.
  5. Serve immediately while the center is still molten.

Pudding

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 3 tbsp sugar (for caramel)
  • 3 eggs
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence

Steps

  1. Melt sugar to a golden caramel and coat the base of a mold.
  2. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, and vanilla essence together.
  3. Pour the mixture over the caramel in the mold.
  4. Steam or bake in a water bath for 30-40 minutes.
  5. Cool, refrigerate, then invert onto a plate to serve.

Pancake

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Steps

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, and baking powder together.
  2. Add egg and milk, whisk until smooth batter forms.
  3. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour a ladle of batter and cook until bubbles form, then flip.
  5. Cook the other side until golden, then serve warm.
